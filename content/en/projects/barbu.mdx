## Result

- A prototype with the full booking flow, history and notifications.
- Free slots in real time, read from Firestore.
- Email confirmation through the n8n webhook and a phone reminder 1 hour before.

Loyalty and Products were left in development. There are no usage metrics.

## How it works

The app saves the booking in Firestore, schedules a reminder on the phone and sends the data to the n8n webhook, which emails the confirmation.

## Context

Barbú is an app prototype for a local barbershop: customers create an account, pick a service, a barber and a free slot, and follow their bookings on their phone.

It was a team project for the Mobile Devices course, built by Carlos, Gustavo Alves, Henrique and Kendy.

## My role

I built most of the app: the Firebase integration (Auth and Firestore), the first screens, login, booking and the n8n integration.

## Account, slots and confirmation

- **Account.** Sign-up creates the user in Firebase Auth and stores name and phone in `users/{uid}`. The Firestore rules let each person read and change only their own data and bookings.
- **Real-time slots.** When a day is picked, the app queries `bookings` for that barber on that date and only shows free slots.
- **On confirm.** The app writes the booking to `bookings`, creates a notice in `notifications/{uid}/items` (shown in the Notices tab), schedules the local reminder and sends the n8n webhook the name, email, date, time, barber and service.
- **Afterwards.** The history lets you cancel (the status changes to "cancelado"), and past bookings show as done.

## Limits and next steps

- Loyalty, Products and the admin panel were left as "coming soon" in the app.
- The webhook points to a local n8n (`localhost`), so the confirmation email only goes out with n8n running on the same machine.
- The slot query doesn't filter by status, so a cancelled slot still shows as booked. And the write doesn't use a transaction: two customers confirming the same slot at the same moment would both be saved. Those are the first fixes I'd make.
